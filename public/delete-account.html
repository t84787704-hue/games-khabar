Account & Data Deletion Request

Developer: Tufail Muhammad Khawaj Muhammad

This page explains how users can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the application and sign in with your account.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your request.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to t84787704@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Data Deletion & Retention Details

  • Data Deleted: User profile details (name, email, avatar), authentication identifiers, tournament history, and in-app currency/wallet balances are permanently deleted within 7 days of verification.
  • Data Retained: Minimal anti-fraud or security logs required for legal or system integrity purposes may be retained for up to 30 days before automatic deletion.
